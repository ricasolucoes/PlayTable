PlayTable brings together sixteen timeless board and card games in a single, lightweight application powered by the Godot engine.

Everything runs entirely on your device. No external servers to query, no accounts to create, no ad SDKs, and zero tracking. Switch your phone to airplane mode and every single game keeps running smoothly.

Board Games Included:
Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Checkers, Chess, Reversi, Nine Men's Morris, Dots and Boxes, Memory Game, Battleship, Mancala, and Ludo.

Card Games Included:
Solitaire (Klondike), Blackjack (Twenty-One), War, Steal the Pile, and Concentration.

What You Will NEVER Find:
No ads, no in-app purchases, no paywalled content, no mandatory logins, and zero telemetry. Your settings and game statistics remain private on your device.

Features clean 3D visuals, responsive touch controls, and runtime language switching (English, Spanish, Portuguese). 100% open-source under the MIT license.
