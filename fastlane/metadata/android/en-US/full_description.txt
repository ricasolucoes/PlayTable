PlayTable collects sixteen classic board and card games in a single app, built with the Godot engine.

Everything runs on the device. There is no server to reach, no account to create, no advertising SDK and no analytics. Open the app on a plane and every game still works.

Board games
Tic-tac-toe, Connect Four, Checkers, Chess, Reversi, Nine Men's Morris, Dots and Boxes, Memory, Battleship, Mancala and Ludo.

Card games
Solitaire, Blackjack, War, Old Maid and Concentration.

What you will not find
No ads, no in-app purchases, no paywalled games, no login, no telemetry. Settings and statistics are stored locally on the device and never leave it.

The interface is available in English, Portuguese and Spanish, switchable at runtime.

The source code is MIT licensed and the game assets are generated procedurally by the app itself, so there are no third-party art or audio files with unclear terms.
