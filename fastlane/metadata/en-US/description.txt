PlayTable brings 22 classic board, card and logic games together in one app: free, ad-free and built for offline play.

Play at your own pace with no account, no in-app purchases and no personal data collection. Settings, statistics and records stay on your device. Local multiplayer works between devices on the same Wi-Fi network, with no server and no sign-up.

BOARD AND LOGIC GAMES
• Checkers, Battleship, Minesweeper and Sudoku.
• Dominoes, Backgammon, Reversi, Ludo and Connect Four.
• Tic-Tac-Toe, Mancala, Senet, Peg Solitaire and Tower of Hanoi.
• Nim and Number Path.

CARD GAMES
• Klondike Solitaire and Spider Solitaire.
• Blackjack, Memory Match, UnoLike and Video Poker.

HIGHLIGHTS
• Play solo against AI or pass-and-play with friends.
• Local multiplayer for Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and Reversi.
• Phone and tablet-friendly interface.
• Portuguese, English and Spanish with in-app language switching.
• Open source, built with Godot.

Take a table full of classics with you, even without an internet connection.
